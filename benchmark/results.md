## Notes

- The per-task budget (`budget_task`) is keyed by the `X-AgentGate-Task` header, which the caller chooses. It limits a cooperative client, not a hostile one: scenario `allow-new-task-new-window` shows a fresh task value getting a fresh window. Only the daily budget and velocity bind a hostile agent.
- Injection scenarios named `*-screen-*` feed a corpus file straight to the output screen; `inject-fake-mcp-tool` goes through the gateway and the fake MCP server.
- The corpus the screen is scored on was written together with its rules, so a clean result shows the rules do what they say, not how an adversary fares.
